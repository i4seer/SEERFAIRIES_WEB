Inside the case

DUNVEGAN HOLLOW, VERMONT • FEBRUARY

THE OFFERING CONTINUED.

THE COMPACT DID NOT.

NOW THE GUARDIAN TAKES.

Fourteen-year-old Eilidh Mòr knows every task on her family's Vermont cattle farm. She can hear a milk bucket filling without looking and find a frozen water line before breakfast. She has never asked why Gran carries yesterday's milk to a hollow stone each dawn.

Then a woman in green appears beside the east wall. The Highland cattle accept her, but a neighbor leaves with grey marks at his throat and a working dog dies at the boundary. The daily offering never stopped. The rest of the old compact did.

With the farm failing, Gran slowing, and the Seer's own power coming due, Eilidh must rebuild the work that once taught the guardian who belonged—and decide whether she will inherit a gift whose costs have already broken the person carrying it.

A guardian needs more than an offering.
