Case overview

DUNVEGAN HOLLOW, VERMONT • THE EAST PASTURE

MILK IN THE STONE.

A PROMISE OLDER THAN THE FARM.

At her family’s Vermont farm, fourteen-year-old Eilidh Mòr helps care for Highland cattle while her grandmother keeps a daily offering at a stone brought from Skye generations ago.

When a stranger arrives on foot with a black notebook, the farm’s oldest custom becomes part of a much larger question: what happens to an agreement when the people who remember it are disappearing?

Some inheritances have to be kept, not owned.
