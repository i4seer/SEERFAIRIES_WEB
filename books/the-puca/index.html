Inside the case

HOLLOW CREEK, WEST VIRGINIA • OCTOBER

THREE ROADS MEET.

THE BLACK HORSE WAITS.

EVERY TRUE ANSWER HAS A PRICE.

Thirteen-year-old Eli Morrow knows every mile around Hollow Creek—until the roads begin folding back to the same crossroads and a black horse with gold eyes starts speaking in his father's voice.

The Púca cannot lie. It does not need to. It can bend distance, borrow memories, and turn the truth Eli has avoided for two years into an offer he may not survive.

With Halloween closing in and the old boundary failing, Eli must face the question without letting the Púca choose the answer.

The truth is not safe just because it is true.
