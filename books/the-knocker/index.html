Case overview

CALUMET RIDGE, MICHIGAN • THE KEWEENAW PENINSULA

THE MINE IS SEALED.

THE TAPPING HAS NOT STOPPED.

Thirteen-year-old Rhys Pascoe hears tapping through the sandstone beneath his home in Calumet Ridge, a Michigan copper town whose mine closed decades ago. The rhythm is too deliberate to be settling rock.

His grandmother Morwenna has kept a small offering in the basement for fifty years. The Cornish stories she carries may explain why the sound has returned—and what the town has forgotten underground.

An old mine can close without ending an old agreement.
