About Seer Fairies

Wonder has a darker edge.

Dark folklore. Forgotten rules. Stories by B. Carter.

The collection

Old stories have
long memories.

Seer Fairies is a collection of dark folkloric mysteries for upper middle-grade and younger teen readers. Each book follows a different creature, a different community, and a young person who begins to notice the signs.

These are the fae of old bargains and dangerous boundaries: the thing at the river, the noise beneath the mine, the presence inside the house. The fear grows from precise details and rules that people have stopped understanding.

Every case stands alone. Read for the mystery, the folklore, or the larger thread of the Seer moving through a world that has forgotten its oldest agreements.

A note from B. Carter

About the author

I write the kind of books I enjoy — full of wonder, big questions, and just enough answers to keep the pages turning.

When I’m not writing, I’m usually reading, walking, or staring at the sky, letting a new idea take shape.

I live with too many books, not enough shelves, and a strong policy of leaving the hall light on.

Thanks for joining the journey. There’s always more to see.

B. Carter
